Local Innovation Fair Draws Record Crowds
Students and entrepreneurs showcase creative solutions for the future.
By Jordan Smith

Hundreds of visitors attended the annual Innovation Fair this weekend, where students, engineers, and entrepreneurs presented projects focused on sustainability, technology, and community development.

Organizers reported the largest turnout in the event's history. Attendees explored interactive exhibits, participated in workshops, and connected with local creators who demonstrated practical solutions to everyday challenges.
